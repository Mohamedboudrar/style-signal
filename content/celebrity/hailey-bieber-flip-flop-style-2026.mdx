The flip-flop is a roughly ten-dollar shoe that has, in the past two decades, occasionally wandered out of its lane and tried to pass for fashion. The attempts usually end in a Vogue trend piece in May, a brief retail run in June, and a quiet retreat to the pool deck by August. The 2026 version of the trend is different, and the difference is mostly Hailey Bieber.

What Bieber is doing with thong sandals in 2026 is small in object but loud in consequence. The shoe is the same one everyone has worn to the beach for forty years. The styling around it is not. The combination — a minimal leather thong, a straight-leg or baggy jean, a clean white shirt, a structured bag, almost no jewelry — has made the flip-flop look like a deliberate choice rather than a concession to the weather. If you have been quietly wondering whether you could actually wear yours to lunch, to a meeting, or to a Saturday of errands and still look like you meant it, the answer this year is yes. The caveat is that the rest of the outfit has to be doing some work.

## Why Hailey Bieber's Flip-Flops Feel Different

The flip-flop has always been an honest shoe. It announces that you are on vacation, that you are five minutes from water, that formality has been officially suspended. The reason it usually fails as a city shoe is not the shoe itself. It is everything that tends to come with it. A flip-flop and a linen cover-up, a flip-flop and a raffia tote, a flip-flop and a sundress with a beach-bag — the combination reads as a single aesthetic gesture, and the gesture is "I am not at work."

Bieber's flip-flops feel different because almost nothing else in the outfit is reading as casual. The jeans are usually a clean, mid-to-light wash, often straight or slightly relaxed, hemmed to graze the ankle. The top is a precise basic — a white ribbed tank, a fitted tee, a button-down with one deliberate roll. The bag is the one giveaway that she is not, in fact, going to the beach: a structured leather shoulder bag or a small top-handle in a saturated color. Hair and makeup are present but unforced. The flip-flop becomes the *only* casual element in a careful outfit, and that asymmetry is what makes the whole thing land.

The same principle works in reverse for the rest of us. A flip-flop in a head-to-toe casual outfit is just a flip-flop. A flip-flop in an otherwise deliberate outfit is a point of view.

## The Flip-Flop Formula She Keeps Repeating

Stripped to its bones, the formula is the same in nearly every repeat: a minimal leather thong sandal in black, brown, or tan; one tailored or relaxed-but-structured bottom; one simple top; one structured bag; and as little jewelry as possible. The repetition is the point. Bieber isn't rotating through a wardrobe of looks — she's rotating through small variations on a single silhouette, and the consistency is what makes the styling read as personal rather than styled.

Three things make the formula hold together. First, the sandal is leather, not rubber, and the toe-post is thin. Second, the silhouette of the clothing is clean — no rips, no distressing beyond a single knee blowout, no oversized logos, no obvious beachwear. Third, the bag is doing a job that a flip-flop cannot do on its own: it is signaling that the wearer is going somewhere with intent. Remove any of those three elements and the outfit tips back into "I just threw this on" territory.

## Pair Them With Relaxed Denim

The denim half of the equation matters more than the sandal half. Flip-flops and skinny jeans have a strange visual effect — the narrow leg makes the foot look unfinished, and the foot makes the leg look stubby. A wide-leg, barrel-leg, or straight-leg jean with a clean hem that hits at the ankle is what allows the thong to do its job without the silhouette collapsing.

The reason is proportion. A relaxed jean creates a column from hip to ankle. The flip-flop then becomes the base of that column rather than an interruption to it. The eye reads the outfit top-to-bottom and finds a deliberate finish at the shoe. A skinny jean interrupts the column about six inches above the ankle, and the thong looks stranded below the break.

Two specifics. The hem should sit at or just above the ankle bone — long enough to graze the top of the foot but not so long that the jean pools on the floor. The wash should be a clean indigo, a clean mid-wash, or a clean black. Heavy distressing, paint splatter, or a visibly worn-in white wash will pull the look back toward the beach cover-up register. A simple, expensive-looking straight-leg jean with a flip-flop is, slightly counterintuitively, more dressed than a fancy sandal with a tired old pair of jeans.

## Why a Kitten Heel Changes Everything

The thong sandal is having a broader moment, and part of that moment is the proliferation of versions that are not, strictly speaking, beach footwear. A kitten-heel thong — a leather flip-flop with a small, two-inch covered heel — is the most useful of these. It looks like a flip-flop. It behaves like a low sandal. The small heel changes the posture, the silhouette, and the formality in ways that matter.

The posture change is the most concrete. A flat flip-flop flattens the calf and shortens the line of the leg. A kitten heel tilts the foot forward, engages the calf, lengthens the line, and shifts the center of gravity up. The visual effect is more "going somewhere" than "standing around." A small heel also makes the thong read as a deliberate footwear choice rather than a default — the kind of shoe you walked into a store and asked for, not the kind you grabbed on the way out of the house.

A kitten-heel thong is the easiest version of the look to commit to. It works in the same outfits — straight-leg denim, a clean top, a structured bag — but it pushes the formality one click further into evening-adjacent territory. If you have a dinner or a more polished daytime plan and you want to wear thong sandals, this is the version that gets you there without a question.

## Make Basic Pieces Look Intentional

The flip-flop is a harsh editor. It does not hide the things that more forgiving shoes can cover. A slouchy hem, a wrinkled tee, an ill-fitting shoulder — all of these become visible when the only other thing happening in the outfit is a leather strap between the toes. This is the actual reason Bieber's flip-flops look good: the rest of the outfit is doing very precise, very simple work.

The basics that survive the flip-flop test are the ones you already own. A white ribbed tank, fitted but not tight, in a cotton with some weight. A clean white button-down, slightly oversized, sleeves pushed to the forearm. A straight-leg or barrel-leg jean in a clean indigo. A black or charcoal trouser with a sharp crease. A simple crewneck in a saturated color. A slip skirt in a silk or a wool. None of these are trend pieces. All of them have been in your closet, or a version of them has, for years.

The accessories around the basics are equally plain. A structured leather bag — top-handle, shoulder, or a small shoulder in a saturated color — in a quality leather. A thin gold or silver chain, possibly stacked once, possibly not. A pair of small hoops or no earrings at all. Sunglasses, if the outfit needs them, in a simple shape. The whole point of the look is that the absence of a "look" is the look. Adding more turns the outfit into a costume of itself.

## How to Wear Flip-Flops Without Looking Like You're Going to the Beach

This is the practical section, and it is the section most people skip. The difference between a flip-flop that looks like a fashion choice and a flip-flop that looks like a beach accessory is almost entirely about the five things you put on with it.

**Choose a leather, not a rubber.** A leather thong — or a high-quality faux leather with a thin, well-finished strap and a clean toe-post — reads as footwear. A rubber flip-flop, especially in a bright color or with a sport sole, reads as pool. The single biggest upgrade any wardrobe can make for this look is replacing the rubber pair with a leather one in a neutral.

**Keep the bag structured.** A structured leather bag — a top-handle, a small shoulder, a boxy tote in a quiet leather — is the strongest single counter-signal to "I'm at the beach." A canvas tote, a raffia bag, a beach tote, or any oversized soft bag tips the outfit back the other way immediately. The bag does about half the work of the whole look.

**Edit the jewelry down.** A flip-flop is a quiet shoe. Stacked bracelets, statement earrings, a chunky necklace, and a layered bag charm all compete with the simplicity of the thong. The best jewelry for this look is small, well-made, and not new. A thin chain, a small pair of hoops, a single ring, possibly a watch. If the jewelry is the loudest thing in the outfit, the flip-flop will not survive.

**Choose fabrics with weight.** A linen trouser and a flip-flop is a beach outfit. A wool trouser and a flip-flop is a fashion outfit. The difference is the fabric. Cotton poplin, wool, structured denim, leather, and heavier silks all hold a clean line. Linen, rayon, and other soft drape fabrics are too suggestive of vacation. A simple swap from a linen pant to a wool or cotton-blend trouser with the same cut will move the entire outfit from cover-up to city.

**Keep the silhouette clean.** A flip-flop cannot compete with ruffles, exaggerated sleeves, peplums, or visible logos. The shoe is doing one job — being a clean, simple interruption to the column of the leg — and the rest of the outfit has to be doing the same job. The strongest flip-flop outfits are the ones where every other piece is also a basic that has been chosen on purpose.

## The Best Clothes to Pair With Them

**Relaxed denim.** Already covered in detail above, but worth restating: a straight-leg, barrel-leg, or baggy jean in a clean wash, hemmed at the ankle, is the single most reliable partner for a flip-flop. The combination works because the proportions match and the fabrics are in the same register.

**Tailored trousers.** A wool or wool-blend trouser in black, charcoal, camel, or cream turns the flip-flop into a city shoe. The trouser does the formality work that the thong cannot do on its own, and the result reads as a deliberate low-shoe choice rather than a casual concession. A high-waisted, slightly wide-leg cut is the strongest silhouette for this pairing.

**Oversized shirts.** A clean oversized button-down, a poplin shirt with the hem half-tucked, or a long-sleeve tee in a heavier cotton is the right top to balance a flip-flop. The volume on top makes the minimal shoe feel intentional, the way a long coat makes a flat boot feel intentional.

**Simple dresses.** A slip dress in a heavier silk, a tank dress in a structured jersey, or a simple shirt-dress in cotton poplin works with a flip-flop when the dress is plain and the fabric is not too soft. A printed maxi, a flowy chiffon, or a beach-cover-up dress does not. The rule is the same as for trousers: if the fabric has weight and the silhouette is clean, the flip-flop will keep up.

**Minimal skirts.** A bias-cut midi, a structured A-line, or a long pleated skirt all work for the same reason a trouser does. The hem should fall below the knee, and the fabric should hold its shape. A short, flippy, or printed skirt will pull the look back into summer-in-the-park territory.

## 5 Hailey-Inspired Outfit Formulas

**1. The Weekend City Walk.** Straight-leg indigo jean, slightly oversized white button-down with the hem half-tucked, flat black leather thong, a small structured shoulder bag in a saturated color (oxblood, butter, or forest green), small gold hoops. Add sunglasses if it's daytime.

**2. The Saturday Lunch.** Baggy or barrel-leg light-wash jean, fitted white ribbed tank, kitten-heel leather thong in tan or brown, a top-handle bag in cream or black, a single thin gold chain. Hair pulled back, no other jewelry.

**3. The Office-to-Evening.** Black or charcoal wool trouser, slightly cropped, a fine merino crewneck in cream or oxblood, flat leather thong in black, a structured shoulder bag, a watch. Swap the thong for a kitten-heel version if the evening is more formal.

**4. The Layered Fall Day.** Straight-leg dark indigo jean, a clean white long-sleeve tee, an unbuttoned oxblood or camel coat, flat leather thong in brown, a small leather crossbody, a single ring. This is the most weather-flexible version of the formula.

**5. The Minimal Summer Evening.** Slip skirt in a heavier silk (oxblood, black, or cream), simple black or white tank, kitten-heel thong in a matching or contrasting leather, a small structured clutch or top-handle bag, thin gold chain, nothing else.

## What to Avoid

The flip-flop is unforgiving, and a few specific pairings will reliably undo the look.

**Overly casual combinations.** A flip-flop with a sweatshirt, a flip-flop with activewear, a flip-flop with a graphic tee, a flip-flop with anything visibly meant for the gym — the shoe will not save the outfit. The flip-flop works when the rest of the outfit is a baseline of "I am a person going about my day." A baseline of "I am a person who just got back from a run" does not.

**Too many beach elements at once.** One beach-coded element is a styling choice. Two is a costume. A flip-flop and a raffia bag is one element too many. A flip-flop and a linen cover-up is two. A flip-flop and a straw hat is three. Pick one casual register and stay in it. The version that works is "I am at home in a city, and the weather is warm." The version that doesn't is "I am at the beach and brought my whole bag."

**Poor proportions.** A flip-flop with a floor-grazing hem, a flip-flop with a cropped ankle that exposes a long bare shin with nothing to anchor it, a flip-flop with a wide-leg pant that puddles on the floor — the proportion logic is the same as for any other shoe, but the flip-flop makes it more visible. The hem has to graze the ankle. The leg has to have a clean line.

**Overly bulky footwear.** A platform flip-flop, a rubber sport thong, a heavily cushioned "recovery" sandal — these are not in the same category as the leather thong. They are a different shoe, and they will pull the outfit into a different register. The look is built around the *minimal* version of the sandal, not the maximalist one.

**Accessories that make the outfit look accidental.** A logo belt, a graphic scarf, a stack of friendship bracelets, a charm bag, novelty sunglasses, a baseball cap — each of these is fine on its own in a different outfit. In a flip-flop outfit, they signal that the rest of the look was not considered. The flip-flop is the most deliberate shoe in your closet, in the sense that it requires the most deliberate styling. Let it be the most interesting thing in the outfit, and the rest of the outfit will follow.

## The Bottom Line

The flip-flop is a small object that asks a large question of the rest of the outfit. The question is: does the rest of the outfit look like you meant it. If yes, the flip-flop is the most interesting shoe you own this summer. If no, no shoe will save it — and the flip-flop will, in fact, be the thing that finally exposes the gap.

The 2026 version of the trend will pass, the way the 2010 and 2005 versions passed. The flip-flop will return to its default life as a pool shoe, and that is where it belongs. In the meantime, the styling lesson is more durable than the trend: a single casual element in a careful outfit reads as confidence. A careful outfit built around a casual element reads as something else. The whole appeal of Hailey Bieber's flip-flops is that they are not a flip-flop so much as they are a small proof that the rest of the look is, in fact, deliberate.

Buy the leather thong. Keep the bag structured. Edit the jewelry down. Hem the jeans. The rest will follow.
